# Cultfit – 4Ps Analysis & Repositioning

### Marketing Management Project

**Course:** Marketing Management

**Instructor:** Dr. Amit Shukla

**Assignment:** Choose a brand, perform a 4Ps analysis, and propose a repositioning strategy.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This repository contains a PowerPoint presentation analyzing **Cultfit**, one of India’s leading fitness and wellness brands. The project covers:

* **4Ps of Marketing (Product, Price, Place, Promotion)**
* **SWOT insights**
* **Cultfit’s current market position**
* **Need for repositioning**
* **Proposed new brand direction and strategies**

The PPT explores how Cultfit currently operates and presents a data-backed repositioning strategy aimed at making Cultfit a **multi-generational, community-driven fitness brand**.

---

## 📊 Key Highlights

* Breakdown of Cultfit’s ecosystem (Cult.fit, Mind.fit, Eat.fit, Care.fit, Cultsport)
* Pricing tiers and membership structure
* Physical & digital distribution strategy
* Digital-first promotion & influencer collaborations
* Proposed repositioning under the new brand promise:
  **“Fitness for All, All for Fitness.”**

---

## 📚 Purpose

This project was created as part of academic coursework to demonstrate practical application of marketing frameworks and strategy development.
